Offline reverse-pinyin data

The included dictionary.tsv is generated by android-tv/tools/build_online_pinyin.py
from the dictionaries distributed in pypinyin 0.55.0. Source inputs are pinned
by SHA-256 in the script. It retains pronunciations (including alternative
readings), converts tone marks to untoned keys, and reverses the mappings.
Candidates are heuristically ordered by primary reading and GB 2312 placement;
there is no claimed word-frequency model.
